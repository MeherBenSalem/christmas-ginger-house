# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- Fixed a crash on Minecraft 26.1.2 and 26.2 (Fabric and NeoForge) when a Gingerbread Man or a
  Reindeer loaded: "Ticking entity", `Can't find attribute minecraft:tempt_range`. On 26.x,
  `TemptGoal` reads the `minecraft:tempt_range` attribute, and neither mob registered it. The
  Reindeer now builds on `Animal.createAnimalAttributes()` and the Gingerbread Man adds the
  attribute, both with the vanilla value of 10.
- Added a Fabric server game test on 26.1.2 and 26.2 that spawns a Reindeer, a Gingerbread Man and
  a Krampus next to a player holding their tempt food and runs their AI. `./gradlew build` runs it.
- 1.20.1 and 1.21.1 do not have the attribute and were not affected; they stay on 2.0.0.

## 2.0.0 "The Christmas Update"

- Every version (1.20.1 Fabric/Forge, 1.21.1, 26.1.2 and 26.2 Fabric/NeoForge) becomes an all-in-one Christmas mod: presents you can wrap and give,
  an advent calendar, a Christmas tree structure, gingerbread men, reindeer (with a red-nosed
  variant), Krampus, gingerbread and candy building blocks, a gingerbread door and trapdoor,
  lights, wreaths, stockings, ornaments, a star topper, festive food, a ginger crop, Santa hat,
  festive sweater, candy cane sword, sleigh bells and a Jingle Bells music disc.
- The gingerbread house is rebuilt from the mod's blocks and decorated.
- All textures, sounds and structures are generated by `tools/assets/build_all.py`.
- Fixed a UTF-8 byte-order mark in the service loader files and `pack.mcmeta` of every root.
- Fixed the house template on 1.21.1 and 26.x living in `data/.../structures`, which 1.21+ no longer reads.
- New listing artwork in `marketing/`.

## 1.1.0

- Public Apache-2.0 MultiLoader rewrite from the original Forge 1.20.1 datapack.
- Added Minecraft 26.1.2 and 26.2 (Fabric + NeoForge).
- Scaffolded 1.20.1 (Fabric + Forge) and 1.21.1 (Fabric + NeoForge).
- Replaced MCreator boilerplate with thin loader entrypoints.
