# Christmas Ginger House 2.0.3

A fix release for Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3.

## Fixed
- Minecraft 26.3: naughty coal burns in furnaces again.
- Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3: wild ginger generates in the world again and can be planted on grass.

## Versions
- 2.0.3 is out for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge), 1.21.1 (Fabric, NeoForge), 26.2 (Fabric, NeoForge) and 26.3 (Fabric, NeoForge). Nothing changes in play on 1.20.1 or 1.21.1.
