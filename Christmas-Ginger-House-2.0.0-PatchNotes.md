# Christmas Ginger House 2.0.0: The Christmas Update

The gingerbread house becomes an all-in-one Christmas mod.

## New
- **Presents** (red, green, blue, gold): wrap up to 9 items, your name goes on the tag, break it to carry it, unwrap with confetti.
- **Advent Calendar**: one door per Minecraft day per player, 24 doors, a gold present on door 24.
- **Christmas tree** structure in snowy biomes and taiga, with lights, ornaments, a star and sealed presents.
- **Creatures**: Gingerbread Man, Reindeer (with the rare red-nosed variant), and Krampus.
- **Decorations**: twinkling Christmas Lights, Wreath, Stocking, Star Topper, three Ornaments.
- **Building blocks**: Gingerbread Block, Gingerbread Bricks (stairs, slab, wall), Frosted Gingerbread Bricks, Gingerbread Door and Trapdoor, Block of Icing, Candy Cane Block, Peppermint Block.
- **Food**: Ginger Root, Gingerbread Dough, Gingerbread, Icing, Gingerbread Cookie, Candy Cane, Hot Cocoa. Ginger crop and wild ginger.
- **Gear**: Santa Hat (3D model), Festive Sweater, Candy Cane Sword, Sleigh Bells, Music Disc "Jingle Bells".
- Naughty Coal fuel, 11 advancements, a creative tab, original sounds.

## Changed
- The gingerbread house is now built from gingerbread, icing and candy blocks, with a decorated porch, presents and loot-table barrels. It spawns more often and adapts to terrain.
- Structure biomes and all spawns are controlled by biome tags.

## Fixed
- Service loader files and `pack.mcmeta` started with a byte-order mark, which stopped the loader platform service from loading. Fixed in every version root.
